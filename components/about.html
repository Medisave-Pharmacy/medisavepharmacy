Our Story

Medisave Pharmacy is operated under Medisave Insaf Sdn Bhd, serving the local community in Batu 4½ Jalan Ipoh, Kuala Lumpur with reliable pharmacy services.

We focus on safe medicine dispensing, clear pharmacist advice, and accessible healthcare products for everyday needs — from prescription medicines to over-the-counter remedies and wellness products.

Our goal is simple: to be the pharmacy our neighbours trust for their family's health.
